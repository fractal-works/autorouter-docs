# Plans and credits

Source: [autorouter.com/pricing](https://autorouter.com/pricing)

AutoRouter uses the AI subscriptions you already pay for first, and pays per
use for everything else. You don't need a subscription to start.

## Which plans can I connect?

Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Gemini plans. Free connects two subscriptions; Pro
connects any number.

Apps that keep their own sign-in still use it: Claude Code's Claude plan
through Claude Code's sign-in, and Codex's ChatGPT plan through Codex's
sign-in. In Cursor, OpenCode and Pi, AutoRouter uses the plans you connect in
AutoRouter. In Claude Desktop, Claude turns run on the Claude accounts you
connect in AutoRouter, not Desktop's own claude.ai sign-in.

## How plans are used

- Your plans go to the work that needs them, so they last longer. Hard work
  goes to the strongest model your plans cover; quick work goes to the
  cheapest capable model.
- When one account reaches its limit, AutoRouter moves on to the account with
  the most room left, before it spends any credit.
- On Pro, when a connected plan hits its limit, turns keep going from the
  monthly included usage until the plan resets. If that runs out too,
  AutoRouter asks you to top up.

Current prices and trial terms are on
[autorouter.com/pricing](https://autorouter.com/pricing). New accounts start
with a week of Pro, or until the included usage is spent, whichever comes
first.

## How paid models are billed

- Paid models are billed at the provider's price plus 10%.
- They draw on included usage first, then on credits you top up.
- Included usage resets each month and doesn't roll over.
- Credits you buy never expire.

## What AutoRouter earns

AutoRouter earns the 10% on paid models and nothing on your plans. Price only
decides between models that already clear the quality bar, and your plans come
first.

## Teams

Enterprise is priced by seats and adds team and seat budgets, allowed models
and defaults, organization-wide privacy rules with zero data retention, spend
by seat, model, team, project or ticket, and an audit log. See
[autorouter.com/enterprise](https://autorouter.com/enterprise) or contact
sales@autorouter.com.
